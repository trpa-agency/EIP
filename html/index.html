Tools & Dashboards

The Lake Tahoe Environmental Improvement Program tracks projects that protect and restore the Tahoe Basin. Use the tools below to explore where EIP projects are happening across the region.

Map

EIP Projects Map

Every EIP project with a known location, on an interactive 2D/3D map. Filter by Focus Area, Stage, watershed, jurisdiction, program, and funding source. Share a link that reproduces your exact view, select projects to export data (CSV, GeoJSON, Shapefile), or render a branded map image for slides.

Open map → Map tool

Project Locator

Make consistent, branded locator maps for presentations and reports. Search an address, APN, or EIP project, pick an EIP or TRPA style and layout, and export a PowerPoint slide or PNG — or start from a selection sent over from the Projects Map.
