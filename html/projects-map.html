Beta — feedback

Every Lake Tahoe EIP project with a known location, colored by Focus Area (derived from the EIP # prefix). Basin-wide projects — research, monitoring, planning without a single point — have no pin but are counted, listed, and included in CSV exports.

  1. Filter — click pills to toggle; double-click one to show only it (double-click again to bring the rest back). More filters — program, jurisdiction, watershed, funding source — live in the collapsible groups; their headings show active counts.
  2. Find & select — search by name or EIP #, click pins or list rows for details, tick rows to build a selection, then Show only selected to preview it.
  3. Export — Data saves GeoJSON + Shapefile + CSV of the selection (or filtered set); Map image renders a branded 16:9 PNG or PowerPoint slide of the current view. Locator map in the selection bar (also the pin button in the header) sends up to 9 picks to the Project Locator for presentation maps.
  4. Share — the button in the header copies a link that reproduces your exact view: filters, selection, and map position.

Click any project for its LakeTahoeInfo page · Data refreshed nightly · Map layers: 2D/3D, clustering, and detailed footprints under the sliders button on the map.
